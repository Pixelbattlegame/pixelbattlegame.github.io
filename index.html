OTHER GAMEMODES

2V2 TEAM DRAW Team up on one big 64×64 canvas. You and your partner draw on it at the same time. The bot judges pick the best team drawing.
CHARADES Up to 8 players take turns drawing a secret word while everyone else types guesses. A new letter of the word shows every 10 seconds. First to guess gets 5 points, then 4, 3, 2, and 1 after that. The drawer gets 1 point for every player who guesses it.
PICASSO MODE 8 artists, a big 64×64 canvas and 10 minutes to draw your masterpiece. Then everyone votes, just like a normal battle.
